Who I am

I'm a MuleSoft Developer at Satya Softtech, based in Hyderabad, India, working since June 2023 on API-led connectivity projects — designing Mule 4 APIs, writing DataWeave transformations for JSON, XML, and CSV payloads, and running CloudHub deployments end to end.
